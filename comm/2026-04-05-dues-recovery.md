## Communication regarding dues recovery

Dear members,

Thank you all for cooperating and paying the monthly dues. Looking for your continued support in helping clear the monthly dues within 2 weeks, once the dues are published. That helps the MC to clear the payments for essential services for the apartment.   

In cases where any member flat is unable to clear their dues even after 2 months, MC will deal with those cases on a case-by-case basis, applying the following general guidelines :    

1. Starting April 1, 2026, if the member flat has not paid their monthly dues starting April 1, 2026 for next two months, following actions may be taken against those member flats -   
    - A fine of 1 K per month levied on their maintenance amount.   
    - The power backup stopped.   
    - Garbage collection discontinued.  

2. The services will be restored if they clear their dues starting from April 1, 2026. (current FY)  
3. Cumulative dues will be computed from April 1, 2025 (Last FY). The finances balances will be carried forward year on year from April 1, 2025.   
4. If the member flat is regularly paying the monthly dues starting April 1, 2026 (current FY), and even if they have their past cumulative dues since FY 2025, no fines or disruption of their services will be done. This is being done on good faith, that the member resident is trying to help and cooperate with the association and being current with their dues, and also trying to clear their past dues.    

As of date, we have the following deficit for the association starting April 1, 2025. We request all the members who are yet to clear their dues, to do it as soon as they can, so that we can continue with our day to day operations.   

![](/assets/images/dues.png)

Going forward the Mana Pristine Maintenance portal - [https://manapristine.github.io/manapristine/](https://manapristine.github.io/manapristine/) will be kept updated with the google sheets, to provide a hassle free, easy and user-friendly experience to the members to view and understand the finances for their respective flats, as well as apartment-wise aggregates. The portal also has the details of all the members who have their dues pending, for their easy reference. The breakup of the maintenance across variable (water expense) and fixed (other expenses) is already provided in the portal for each flat, and also for apartment-wise aggregate.   

The process in which this portal is being updated is as follows -   

1. Treasurer continues to use the google sheets to compute the monthly maintenance.   
2. Once the maintenance amount is tallied for collection and expenses for a given month, the treasurer takes a snapshot of the google sheet, exports it to excel and configures it github.   
3. Then from that snapshot of the google sheet taken the portal is refreshed to visualize the latest data for the consumption of the members and the occupants of their flats.   
4. For every month's computation of maintenance, the same process is followed. This ensures a proper backup-snapshot of the financial books at the end of every month and a traceability that can always be referred to in future.   
5. Every member and their occupants can always generate pdf reports and keep with them for every month, along with apartment-wise aggregates.  

Please keep letting MC know if you have any other suggestions to improve the communication, ease of use, and support to the members.
