📋 Community Library Guidelines

💡 How to Borrow a Book

  1. Visit the Mana Pristine Clubhouse / Library Shelf.
  2. Pick your desired title and note down the Book Sr# & your Flat# in the reading register.
  3. Borrow up to 2 books at a time for up to 14 days.
  4. Handle with care and return promptly for fellow neighbors to enjoy.

Have books you'd like to share? Contact the Management Committee to donate books to the community library!
